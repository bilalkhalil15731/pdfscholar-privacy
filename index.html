This Privacy Policy explains how the PDF Scholar mobile application ("the App", "we", "us") handles information. PDF Scholar is an offline PDF library and reader app. Please read this policy carefully to understand our practices.

1. No Accounts or Sign-In

PDF Scholar does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide any personal identity information to use the App. Everyone who uses the App on a given device has the same access — there are no user profiles, passwords, or login credentials of any kind.

2. Your Documents Stay On Your Device

When you add a PDF to your library, the App copies that file into the App's private storage on your own device. This happens entirely offline. PDF Scholar does not upload, transmit, back up, or otherwise send your documents, book titles, folders, or reading activity to any server operated by us or anyone else. We do not have access to your library or its contents.

3. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, request, or store any of the following:

  • Your name, email address, or other contact details
  • Your precise or approximate location
  • Contacts, calendar, camera, or microphone data
  • Payment or financial information
  • The content of the PDF files you add to your library

4. Advertising

PDF Scholar is supported by advertising delivered through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google AdMob collects certain information from your device, including your Advertising ID and other device and app-interaction identifiers, which may be used to serve ads, measure ad performance, and, depending on your ad settings, personalize the ads you see. This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us directly.

You can review how Google collects and uses this data, and manage your ad personalization settings, here:

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • Google Ads Settings — manage ad personalization

5. Third-Party Services

The only third-party service integrated into PDF Scholar is Google AdMob (for advertising). We do not use analytics SDKs, crash-reporting services that transmit personal data, or any other third-party data-collection tools beyond what is described above.

6. Children's Privacy

PDF Scholar is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the App requires no account and stores no personal data on our servers, no server-side child data exists to be collected or retained.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Since PDF Scholar has no accounts and no backend server, we do not retain any of your data after it leaves your device — there is nothing on our end to delete. All app data (your library, settings, and cached files) is stored locally and is removed automatically if you uninstall the App or clear its storage from your device settings.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the App or legal requirements. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent revision. Continued use of the App after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact us at: pdfscholar.app@gmail.com
